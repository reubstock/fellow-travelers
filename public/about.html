What We Do

In 2025, a group of friends had dinner. The AI talk ran out quickly. What was left — honesty, curiosity, a sense that something was missing — was more interesting.

We kept meeting. Members now include people from a range of fields and places, united less by what they do than by how they think.

No hierarchy, no dues, no agenda. The only commitment is to show up and be honest.
